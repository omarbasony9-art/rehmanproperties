5. DEAL CALCULATIONS
==================================================

When both:

estimatedMarketValue
upsetAmount

exist, calculate:

estimatedSpread =
estimatedMarketValue - upsetAmount

discountPercent =
((estimatedMarketValue - upsetAmount) / estimatedMarketValue) * 100

equityMultiple =
estimatedMarketValue / upsetAmount

Round discountPercent to 1 decimal.
